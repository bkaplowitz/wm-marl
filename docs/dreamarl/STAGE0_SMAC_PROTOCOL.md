## 0B — Dual outcome instrumentation

The environment continues to expose the pinned SMAC-v1 scalar reward, broadcast
over agents, as `reward`. The learner, actor, replay, checkpoint parameter tree,
and optimization objective are therefore unchanged.

The adapter now logs two separate returns:

- `legacy_return` is the exact scaled reward returned by pinned SMAC-v1. With
  positive-only dense reward, that implementation applies `abs()` to the net
  enemy health-plus-shield delta and can therefore reward shield regeneration.
- `corrected_return` uses the same death bonus, win bonus, and scale, but replaces
  the legacy damage term with

  `sum_e max(H_prev[e] + S_prev[e] - H[e] - S[e], 0)`.

The corrected value is diagnostic only. It is not substituted into training and
must not be presented as the published SMAC reward.

Every fixed evaluation additionally reports:

- win rate and timeout rate;
- enemy damage, health damage, shield damage, and shield regeneration;
- enemy/allied deaths and final survivors;
- no-op, stop, movement, and attack fractions;
- target-switch counts and attack-target distribution;
- the mean and spread of the legacy/corrected return gap.

Win rate is authoritative. Legacy return is retained for historical benchmark
comparability; corrected outcomes diagnose whether dense reward corresponds to
actual combat progress.

## Verification

- Focused adapter, evaluation, configuration, and launcher contract checks pass.
- A real SC2 4.10 `3m` episode emitted coherent deaths, survivors, timeout, and
  action counts.
- The completed 100k `3m` seed-123 checkpoint restored without parameter changes
  and completed 32 deterministic episodes under the new evaluator.
- That checkpoint produced 0/32 wins, 100% timeouts, mean legacy/corrected return
  2.5925, mean enemy damage 44.81, mean enemy deaths 0.25, and mean enemy
  survivors 2.75. As expected on shield-free `3m`, the legacy/corrected gap was
  exactly zero.
- The completed 100k `3s_vs_4z` seed-123 checkpoint produced 3/32 wins and 84.4%
  timeouts. Mean legacy return was 19.67 while corrected return was 15.95: the
  legacy signal was inflated by 3.72 points (18.9%). It caused real but incomplete
  combat progress—1.625/4 enemy deaths and 2.375 enemy survivors on average—while
  enemy shields regenerated by 214.89 points per episode. Thus shield reward is a
  material confound, but not the entire hard-map failure.

Stage 0 stops here. Actor stabilization and any counterfactual or joint-model
work require a separate Stage 1 decision.
